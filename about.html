ABOUT SIGNY

Products chosen with purpose.

We believe everyday products can be practical, well-made and enjoyable — without making life unnecessarily complicated.

OUR STORY

Building a brand around everyday usefulness.

SIGNY International is built around a simple idea: find products that solve real everyday needs and make them accessible to more people.

Our range spans artisan collections, kitchen accessories, gardening, home improvement and kids' products. As we grow, we also work to bring attention to products and craftsmanship from India and help them reach customers more widely.

We are building SIGNY step by step — with an emphasis on quality, value, customer experience and long-term trust.

WHAT MATTERS TO US

Our principles.

Useful by design

Products should have a clear purpose and make a meaningful difference in everyday life.

Quality with value

We look for the right balance between dependable quality and accessible pricing.

Growing together

We value relationships with customers, suppliers, makers and artisans as we build the brand.

INDIAN CRAFTSMANSHIP

Helping good work travel further.

We believe India's traditional skills and craftsmanship deserve opportunities to reach customers beyond their immediate markets. SIGNY's journey includes exploring ways to connect authentic products and makers with customers across the country and globally.
